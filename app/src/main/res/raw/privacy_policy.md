# Privacy Policy

**Custom AppLock Studio** is built to work fully offline. Your privacy comes first.

## What stays on your device
- Your lock passcodes are stored only on this device, as salted one-way hashes. They can never be read back.
- Settings are kept in encrypted local storage.
- Intruder photos and the attempt log are saved in private app storage and never uploaded.

## Accessibility service
- The accessibility service is used only to detect which app has just opened, so a lock screen can be shown.
- It does not read screen content, keystrokes, messages or passwords, and it sends nothing anywhere.

## Permissions
- Draw over other apps: show the lock screen instantly.
- Notifications: keep the protection service alive.
- Camera (optional): take an intruder selfie after wrong attempts.
- Biometrics (optional): unlock with fingerprint or face.

## Ads
- The main app is ad free.
- A single banner ad is shown on the lock screen only. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers under Google's own privacy policy.
